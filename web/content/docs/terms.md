## The short version

Use SkillIssues to find and solve open-source issues, and be decent about it.

## The service

SkillIssues is free. It is provided as it is, with no promise that it will always be available, accurate or complete. We may change it, pause it or stop it at any time.

The issue and repository data comes from GitHub and can be out of date, wrong or missing. Check an issue on GitHub before you work on it.

## Your account

- You sign in with your GitHub account. Keep it secure.
- You are responsible for what is done through your account.
- You can delete your account at any time. See [Privacy policy](/privacy).

## Other people's work

Issues, repositories and their content belong to their authors on GitHub. SkillIssues links to them. When you contribute, follow each project's own license, contribution rules and code of conduct. SkillIssues has no say in whether a project accepts your pull request.

## Acceptable use

Don't:

- break into, overload or probe the service, or try to get around its limits;
- scrape beyond what the published [API](/docs/public-api) and its rate limits allow;
- use SkillIssues to spam maintainers, for example by commenting on or claiming many issues you don't intend to work on;
- use themes to mislead other people, hide information, or try to run code or load content from elsewhere;
- post unlawful or abusive content, including in a theme's name or description.

We may limit or remove access, or delete content, if you break these terms or put the service or others at risk.

## Themes

A theme you create belongs to you. If you make a theme public, you let anyone who has its link view it and apply it. You can make it private or delete it again.
