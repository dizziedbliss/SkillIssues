## Is it free?

Yes.

## Do I need an account?

No, not to browse. Signing in with GitHub lets you save issues and set the languages and level you're looking for, and it lets the signed-in app keep your themes.

## What can SkillIssues do with my GitHub account?

Nothing. Sign-in only reads who you are (your GitHub id, username and avatar), and we throw the token away. We can't see your private repositories or act on your behalf. A SkillIssues session ends when you sign out, when it expires, or when you sign out everywhere. Revoking the app on GitHub doesn't end one, because there is no token to revoke.

## Why isn't a repository I know listed?

It may be under the star floor, inactive for two years, archived, a fork, or without a main language. The repository lookup on the home page tells you which. See [Suggest a repository](/docs/suggest-a-repository).

## Why does an issue say "unrated"?

Its maintainers haven't labelled its difficulty. We don't guess. See [Difficulty ratings](/docs/difficulty-ratings).

## How fresh is the data?

Changes on GitHub usually show here within two hours. See [How it works](/docs/how-it-works).

## Why do I see the same few repositories?

The default feed gives each repository one slot and redraws the order daily, so the top should change from day to day. Filters or a language preference narrow the pool, which can make repeats more likely. See [Finding issues](/docs/finding-issues).

## Why does a page of results sometimes look short, or empty?

When you browse the shuffled feed with filters that few repositories match, the catalog may stop looking after a couple of seconds and give you a short page along with a way to continue. That isn't the end of the results. The website keeps reading a few pages before it tells you nothing matches. If you use the API directly, see [Public API](/docs/public-api).

## An issue I opened or closed isn't showing the change.

Give it a couple of hours. If it's still wrong after that, [report a bug](/docs/report-a-bug) with the issue's link.

## Can I use SkillIssues without the desktop app?

Yes. The desktop app only handles Git for you. See [The desktop app](/docs/desktop-app).

## Is there a leaderboard, or XP?

Not yet. See [Leaderboard](/docs/leaderboard).

## Can I change how the app looks?

Yes, with themes. See [Themes](/docs/themes).

## Can I delete my account?

Yes. Deleting an account removes your profile, preferences, saved issues and themes. See [Privacy policy](/docs/privacy) for how to ask.

## How do I report a security problem?

Privately. Don't open a public issue. See [Security](/docs/security).
